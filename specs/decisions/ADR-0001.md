# ADR-0001 — PetID is soulbound

**Status** accepted · **Date** 2026-08-15 · **Decider** PawChain ID architect

## Context

An identity that can be sold separates the animal from the household answerable for it.
Responsibility is the thing being tracked, and responsibility is not an asset. If a PetID
can be listed and bought, then the binding the whole system exists to create can be
dissolved with a transaction, and the deposit becomes a tradeable liability rather than a
commitment.

## Options considered

1. **Transferable ERC-721** — simple, well understood, enormous tooling support, and it
   immediately defeats the purpose.
2. **Soulbound token plus an explicit two-party transfer ceremony** — non-transferable by
   sale, but re-homing remains possible through a deliberate protocol step.
3. **Off-chain record only** — no token at all, a signed append-only log.

## Decision

Option 2.

## Because

Responsibility must not be tradeable, but legitimate re-homing must remain possible and
auditable. Cats are re-homed for ordinary reasons — emigration, allergy, death in the
family — and a system that cannot express that will be routed around. A ceremony
requiring both parties' keys makes transfer a deliberate, recorded act rather than a
market transaction.

Option 1 was rejected on purpose rather than on cost: it is the easiest thing to build and
the wrong thing to build. Option 3 is revisited in ADR-0002 on different grounds.

## Consequences

- Transfer becomes a protocol step with its own threat surface — see threat model row 7,
  "transfer laundering". A cooling period and a transfer history are now required
  (REQ-014).
- We give up the entire NFT tooling ecosystem: wallets, marketplaces and explorers all
  assume transferability, so anything that displays a PetID has to be built.
- A lost household key becomes a support problem rather than a market problem. There is no
  recovery path designed yet; recorded as part of OQ-002.
